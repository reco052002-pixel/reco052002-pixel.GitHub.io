05 / FAQ
Do I need sprint experience?

No. Sprint 101 is designed specifically for beginners, and the other sessions can be adjusted to your level.

Do I need to be fast already?

No. The point of the training is to help you improve from your current level.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes, running shoes, water, and enough time to warm up and train properly.
